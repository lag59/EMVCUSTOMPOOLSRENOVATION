About EMV

Built With Craftsmanship. Restored With Purpose.

At EMV Custom Pools & Restoration, we believe a pool should be more than a place to swim—it should be a space where families gather, relax, and create lasting memories.

We specialize in custom pool construction, pool renovations, restoration, hardscapes, structural improvements, and outdoor transformations designed to bring beauty, functionality, and lasting value to every property we work on.

From the first stages of excavation and structural work to tile, coping, plaster, decking, and final finishes, our team approaches every project with attention to detail and a commitment to quality craftsmanship.

Custom Pools Designed Around You

No two properties are the same, and no two families use their outdoor space in exactly the same way. That is why we focus on creating customized solutions based on each client's property, vision, lifestyle, and goals.

Whether you are building a brand-new pool, updating an outdated backyard, repairing structural components, replacing finishes, or completely transforming an existing pool, EMV works to create a finished space that feels intentional and built to last.

More Than Pool Construction

Our experience extends beyond the pool itself. EMV Custom Pools & Restoration provides a range of services that can help transform the entire outdoor environment, including:

  • Custom pool construction
  • Pool renovation and restoration
  • Pool plaster and resurfacing
  • Tile and coping installation
  • Travertine and pool decking
  • Concrete and masonry work
  • Retaining walls
  • Pool plumbing
  • Structural pool repairs
  • Site preparation, grading, and backfill
  • Specialty pool features
  • Outdoor renovation projects

Restoration That Brings New Life to Existing Pools

An older pool does not always need to be replaced. Sometimes the right restoration can completely change the appearance, performance, and usability of an existing space.

We help homeowners restore aging pools through resurfacing, plaster replacement, tile and coping upgrades, structural repairs, plumbing improvements, deck renovations, and complete visual transformations.

Quality You Can See

The work behind a beautiful finished pool matters just as much as the final appearance. That is why we take pride in every stage of the process—from excavation, plumbing, reinforcement, and structural preparation to masonry, decking, finishes, and final details.

We believe quality workmanship should be visible not only when the project is completed, but throughout the construction process.

Our Commitment

At EMV Custom Pools & Restoration, our commitment is simple:

Do the work correctly. Communicate clearly. Pay attention to the details. Deliver a finished product we can be proud to put our name on.

We understand that pool construction and restoration are significant investments. Our goal is to provide our clients with confidence throughout the project and a finished outdoor space they can enjoy for years to come.
